Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Your privacy matters.
This Privacy Policy explains what information HOSHINO may collect, how that information may be used, and the choices available to users.

1. Introduction

HOSHINO is a community-driven online gaming and development project. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you use HOSHINO websites, services, games, accounts, development tools, APIs, and community features.

This policy should be read together with the HOSHINO Terms of Use.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, HOSHINO may collect or receive different categories of information.

  • Account information.
  • Username and profile information.
  • Game and gameplay information.
  • Technical information.
  • Device and browser information.
  • IP address and approximate network information.
  • Information you voluntarily submit.
  • Reports and moderation information.

HOSHINO does not necessarily collect every category listed above from every user.

3. Account Information

If accounts are supported, HOSHINO may process information associated with your account, such as your username, account identifier, authentication information, profile information, and account settings.

Authentication information should be protected and should not be publicly shared.

4. Technical Information

When you access HOSHINO, technical information may be processed to operate and secure the service.

This may include IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, connection information, error information, and basic server logs.

5. User Content

HOSHINO may process content that you voluntarily submit, including usernames, game creations, scripts, images, text, audio, videos, comments, and other material.

User Content may be processed so that HOSHINO can display, store, transmit, moderate, and operate the content as part of the service.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate HOSHINO services.
  • Maintain accounts and authentication.
  • Provide games and interactive features.
  • Maintain security.
  • Detect abuse and malicious activity.
  • Investigate reports and violations.
  • Debug technical problems.
  • Improve reliability and functionality.
  • Communicate important service information.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Security

HOSHINO may use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Information Sharing

HOSHINO may share information when reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, provide requested features, comply with legal obligations, or protect users and infrastructure.

HOSHINO does not intend to sell personal information as a general business practice.

9. Third-Party Services

HOSHINO may rely on third-party providers for hosting, communications, authentication, analytics, infrastructure, or other technical services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Cookies & Storage

HOSHINO may use cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar technologies when necessary to provide features, remember settings, maintain sessions, or improve security.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies and local storage.

11. Communications

HOSHINO may communicate with users about service updates, security matters, account activity, maintenance, or other important information.

Community communications may also occur through third-party platforms such as Discord.

12. Children & Minors

HOSHINO is intended to operate in accordance with applicable age and privacy requirements.

Where applicable, additional protections may apply to children and minors.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has provided information inappropriately may contact HOSHINO through an official community channel.

13. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce rules, comply with legal requirements, or maintain appropriate technical records.

14. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have legal rights relating to personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or otherwise control certain information.

These rights may be subject to legal limitations and exceptions.

15. Account & Data Deletion

Where account deletion is supported, users may request deletion of their account and associated information through an available official HOSHINO support channel.

Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

16. International Processing

HOSHINO and its service providers may operate infrastructure in different countries or regions.

As a result, information may be processed outside the country where you live, subject to applicable legal requirements.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as HOSHINO develops.

When significant changes are made, HOSHINO may provide notice through the website, community channels, or another reasonable method.
